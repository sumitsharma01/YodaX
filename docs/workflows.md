# Using the workspace

## Daily forecasts

Choose a supported dashboard stock to inspect its saved next-session forecast, historical prices and settled results. Currency selection changes display values, not the underlying USD prediction. Refresh checks for newly published data.

## Future Market

Ask a research question, follow its sources and inspect the evidence map. A supported numerical request appears in the requested-predictions section. It can remain queued until the scheduled worker has complete inputs. The saved result records its own cutoff and target; the original question time is not the inference cutoff.

Export a research report when the answer is complete. The report includes the research context and references. Conditional outlooks are labelled separately from numerical forecasts.

## Methodology and evaluation

Use methodology to inspect evidence of settled forecasts and adaptive updates. The private evaluation page is for candidate comparisons and worker diagnostics. Pending forecasts need their target session to close before they can be scored.

Historical replay gives earlier evidence without waiting, but cannot replace prospective checks of data arrival, issuance timing and live reliability. A five-session horizon also creates overlapping outcomes, so five rows do not mean five independent experiments.
